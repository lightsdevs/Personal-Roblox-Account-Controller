# Personal Roblox Account Controller (PRAC)

Manage your Roblox accounts in one place, switch between them, and join games with multiple accounts.

Inspired by [Roblox Account Manager](https://github.com/ic3w0lf22/Roblox-Account-Manager).

## Using the app

- **Add accounts:** Click **Add / sign in again** and sign in on Roblox's login page. Use the same button if an account needs you to sign in again.
- **Select accounts:** Click an account, hold Ctrl to select more, or hold Shift to select a range.
- **Limit background accounts.** Select your alts and check **Launch as background** once. They share 15% of total CPU by default. In **Settings**, adjust **Background CPU cap** and click **Apply CPU cap** to update running and future background clients. Leave your main accounts unchecked. This uses the standard Windows Roblox Player installation.
- **Join games:** Enter a game's place ID (the number in its Roblox link) or a player's username, then join with your selected accounts.
- **Trim background RAM.** When your alts are idle, click **Settings → Trim background RAM** to ask Windows to reclaim resident memory from running PRAC background clients. They can use the memory again as needed.
- **Save favorites:** Click **Save game** to add a game to **Saved games** for easy access later.
- **Add notes:** Enter a description and click **Save description** to apply it to your selected accounts.
- **Browse Roblox:** Select one account and click **Open account browser**.
- **Settings:** Switch between dark and light mode, import accounts from an old CSV file, or enable multiple Roblox windows with the singleton toggle. Enable it before opening Roblox; if you see a warning, close Roblox and restart the controller.

Your saved account sign-ins are encrypted on your computer, nothing leaves your system.
